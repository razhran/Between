AETHER // The Liquid Web Experiment

Traditional websites are rigid monuments of pre-built buttons designed for an imaginary average user. AETHER reverses that paradigm: it begins as an empty breathing void with zero pre-existing UI, synthesizing a bespoke, temporary micro-application tailored strictly to your current intent—and evaporating once your moment passes.
